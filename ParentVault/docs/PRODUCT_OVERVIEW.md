# ParentVault Product Overview

## One-liner

ParentVault is a secure Android/iOS family operations vault for child profiles, schedules, medication reminders, AI-assisted imports, and factual journaling.

## MVP promise

Help a parent stop losing critical child information across screenshots, texts, calendars, notes, and memory — while keeping AI suggestions reviewable and sensitive data protected.

## Product pillars

1. **Protect the child first:** security, privacy, masking, export, and deletion are product features.
2. **Make the week easier:** today/next-up, reminders, and medication confirmations matter more than broad feature count.
3. **Turn chaos into structure:** imports and chat should convert messy inputs into reviewed records.
4. **Respect parent control:** cloud-first convenience now, self-host path later.
