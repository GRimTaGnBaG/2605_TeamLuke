# ParentVault Monetization and Positioning

## Positioning

**ParentVault is a private command center for child information, parenting schedules, medication reminders, AI-assisted imports, and factual family journaling.**

It is not a legal app, not a medical app, and not a co-parent fight app. The product should feel calm, secure, and practical: a vault that helps parents execute the week.

## Primary audience

- Single parents and co-parents managing complex schedules.
- Parents of children with medications, therapies, school events, or custody rotations.
- Privacy-conscious parents who want better structure than notes, screenshots, and scattered calendars.

## Core differentiation

1. **Security-first child vault:** designed around sensitive child data from day one.
2. **AI with approval:** imports messy real-world documents, but the parent remains in control.
3. **Operational focus:** today, reminders, medication confirmations, and journaling in one place.
4. **Cloud convenience now, self-host later:** easy start with a path to parent-controlled infrastructure.

## Suggested pricing

### Free trial

- 14–30 day trial.
- One parent account.
- One or two child profiles.
- Limited imports and attachment storage.

### Parent Plus — $6–9/month

- Multiple child profiles.
- Unlimited schedules and journal entries.
- Medication reminders and taken history.
- Basic AI import credits.
- Export/delete controls.

### ParentVault Pro — $12–19/month

- Higher AI import limits.
- More attachment storage.
- Advanced exports.
- Future caregiver/co-parent sharing.
- Priority support.

### Self-host license — later

- One-time license or annual maintenance.
- Optional cloud relay/backup add-on.
- Best positioned after the cloud product proves retention.

## Launch wedge

Start with the high-pain weekly loop:

1. Add child profile and meds.
2. Add custody/school/events.
3. Receive reminders.
4. Mark medication taken.
5. Journal factual events.
6. Import the next messy screenshot/flyer into the schedule.

If this loop works, monetization can follow. Avoid leading with broad “family management” language; lead with relief from missed reminders, scattered screenshots, and fragile memory.

## Trust messaging

- “AI suggests. You approve.”
- “Sensitive fields are masked and protected.”
- “Your child data is exportable and deletable.”
- “Cloud-first for convenience. Self-host path for control.”

## Risks to avoid

- Overpromising legal usefulness.
- Appearing to encourage custody conflict.
- Sending sensitive data to AI without explicit consent.
- Hiding data retention, deletion, or pricing details.
- Launching without a clear support and incident response process.

## Early validation metrics

- Activation: parent creates first child profile and first reminder.
- Weekly retention: parent opens today/next-up at least three days in a week.
- Reminder value: medication or event reminders marked useful/taken.
- Import value: percent of import suggestions accepted after review.
- Trust: export/delete/privacy screens viewed without support escalations.
- Willingness to pay: trial-to-paid conversion and cancellation reasons.
