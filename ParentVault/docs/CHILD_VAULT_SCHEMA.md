# Detailed Child Vault Schema

ParentVault should be detailed enough that a parent can answer real-life questions quickly, without hunting through text messages, papers, portals, or memory. The chatbot should proactively help collect details parents may not think to add.

## Child identity

- Display name
- Legal name
- Preferred name
- Birthdate
- SSN last four / encrypted SSN
- Notes

## Medical profile

- Allergies
- Medical conditions
- Blood type
- Dietary restrictions
- Sensory needs
- Care instructions
- Immunization notes
- Medications:
  - Name
  - Dosage
  - Route
  - Instructions
  - Schedule
  - Active/inactive
  - Start/end date
  - Prescribing provider
  - Pharmacy where medication is filled
  - Encrypted Rx number
  - Refill instructions
  - Refills remaining
  - Last filled date
  - Next refill due date
  - Side effects to watch

## Doctors, pharmacies, and care providers

Each provider record supports:

- Type: pediatrician, doctor, dentist, specialist, therapist, pharmacy, school, childcare, insurance, legal, other
- Provider/doctor/pharmacy name
- Role
- Office or organization name
- Phone
- After-hours phone
- Email
- Portal URL / refill app URL
- Office address/location
- Office hours
- Notes
- Primary provider flag
- Accepts electronic prescriptions
- Preferred for refills

Example questions this enables:

- “Who is her pediatrician?”
- “What is the doctor’s phone number?”
- “Where is the dentist office?”
- “Where do we fill her medication?”
- “What is the pharmacy phone number?”
- “When is the next refill due?”

## School and childcare

- School name
- District
- Grade
- Teacher
- Main phone
- Attendance phone
- Website/calendar URL
- Address/location
- Office hours
- School hours
- Pickup instructions
- Bus info

## Insurance

- Insurance provider name
- Plan name
- Policy holder
- Relationship to child
- Encrypted member ID
- Encrypted group number
- Encrypted Rx BIN
- Encrypted Rx PCN
- Encrypted Rx group
- Main phone
- Nurse line
- Pharmacy benefits/Rx phone
- Portal URL
- Card front/back image URI
- Copay notes
- Prior authorization notes
- Notes

Example questions this enables:

- “Who is the child’s insurance provider?”
- “What is the insurance phone number?”
- “Is there a pharmacy benefits number?”
- “Who is the policy holder?”

## Bot completeness prompts

The app should not rely on parents to know every field. The chatbot should detect gaps and ask helpful follow-ups:

- “Who is the child’s insurance provider?”
- “Is there a pharmacy benefits number on the insurance card?”
- “Where do they fill medication?”
- “What is the pharmacy phone number and address?”
- “Does the pharmacy have a refill app or portal?”
- “Who prescribed this medication?”
- “How many refills are left?”
- “When was it last filled?”
- “Who is allowed to pick up the child from school?”

These prompts are generated by `packages/shared/src/completeness.ts`.

## Custody/legal

- Court
- Encrypted case number
- Decree date
- Custody summary
- Exchange rules
- Holiday rules
- Source document IDs
- Notes

The app should present custody/legal content as stored facts only, not legal advice.

## Emergency contacts

- Name
- Relationship
- Phone
- Email
- Address
- Allowed pickup flag
- Notes

## Schedules and journal

Schedules and journal entries link back to the child and, where useful, to provider IDs, medication IDs, school dates, or source document IDs. This lets the assistant answer from relationships, not just raw text.
